# The AI Engineering Skills Map — Part 2: Building and Deploying AI Applications

**Author:** Andrew Ng  
**Source:** DeepLearning.AI  

## Summary

Building AI applications requires knowing how AI models work and how to use them properly. Unlike normal software, AI can give different answers even when asked similar questions, so it needs to be tested and improved regularly. AI models also need good and correct information to give useful answers. Developers can build AI agents that use different tools to carry out tasks. They must check the results, find mistakes, and fix them to make their applications better. They also need to make sure their applications are safe, fast, and not too expensive to run. Understanding machine learning helps developers know how AI learns and makes decisions. In the end, building good AI applications takes practice, patience, regular testing, and a willingness to keep learning.
