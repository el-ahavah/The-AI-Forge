# The AI Engineering Skills Map — Part 5: Shaping the Build

**Author:** Andrew Ng  
**Source:** DeepLearning.AI  

## Summary

AI engineers do more than write code. They also help decide what to build and how to make it useful. They do not always wait for others to tell them what to do. They take part in planning projects, solving problems, and making decisions. They build small parts of a project, test them, listen to users, and make improvements. They also try to understand what people need and make sure the product is easy to use. Working with others is important too, so good communication helps them share ideas and work as a team. AI engineers should take responsibility for their work, find problems, and look for ways to solve them. They must also keep learning because AI technology is always changing. This helps them become better at their work and build useful products.
